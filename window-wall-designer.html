Window Wall Designer

Click the hatched pocket to place a unit. Split and drag the mullion to size. The opening never changes.
Planning aid only. Unit sizes = opening − bucks − mullions. Verify with the manufacturer and the AHJ before ordering or submitting. Not for construction.
×

How to use it

1. Type the opening. Inches (120) or feet-inches (10'-0"). The drawing and PDF always print in inches. The opening is the hole in the wall — it does not change when you add bucks or mullions.

2. Place a unit by clicking the hatched pocket (or Add unit). Split vertical for side-by-side, split horizontal for stacked. Drag the gold handles or the mullion bar — sizes snap to 1".

3. Pick the type on the selected unit: Fixed, Single Hung, Horizontal Roller, Casement, Swing Door, Sliding Glass Door.

4. Print / save a labeled shop drawing. Mullion stock is actual thickness (2x4 = 1.5"). Bucks wrap all four sides and shrink the pocket.
